# Threat-Control-Evidence Map
> **Artifact type:** TEMPLATE  
> **Completion status:** Blank for reuse  
> **Required for:** Full Assurance Lifecycle

## What this document is

A traceability record connecting threats and failure modes to assets, consequences, security requirements, controls, evidence, owners, validation, and decisions.

## Who should complete it

System owners, threat modelers, security engineers, operators, risk owners, and reviewers.

## When to use it

Use after defining the system boundary and identity model, and update it when threats, architecture, controls, suppliers, or operating assumptions change.

## Decision supported

Whether material threats and failure modes are addressed by evidenced, validated, owned, and recoverable controls.

## Evidence Sufficiency Review

For every material row, confirm:

- [ ] The threat or failure mode is described at a useful level.
- [ ] The affected asset or decision is explicit.
- [ ] Mission or business consequence is stated.
- [ ] Uncertainty and assumptions are visible.
- [ ] At least one relevant control category is identified.
- [ ] Controls have named owners.
- [ ] Evidence is linked rather than merely asserted.
- [ ] Validation status is stated.
- [ ] Corrective and recovery paths exist where required.
- [ ] Residual risk has an accountable owner.
- [ ] The decision is bounded to the current stage and evidence.
