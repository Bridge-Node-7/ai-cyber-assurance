
# References

Authoritative public references supporting the repository's assurance, engineering, AI, incident-response, Zero Trust, secure-development, and public-safety approach.

Publication status should be rechecked before each major release.

## Cybersecurity Risk and Systems Engineering

### NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0

- **NIST CSWP 29**, *The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0*
- Status: Final
- Published: February 2024
- Source: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/cswp/29/the-nist-cybersecurity-framework-csf-20/final
- Relevance: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover outcomes.

### NIST SP 800-160 Vol. 1 Rev. 1

- *Engineering Trustworthy Secure Systems*
- Status: Final
- Published: November 2022
- Source: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/160/v1/r1/final
- Relevance: Systems security engineering, trustworthiness, lifecycle, and assurance.

### NIST SP 800-160 Vol. 2 Rev. 1

- *Developing Cyber-Resilient Systems: A Systems Security Engineering Approach*
- Status: Final
- Published: December 2021
- Source: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/160/v2/r1/final
- Relevance: Anticipate, withstand, recover, and adapt.

### NIST SP 1308

- *NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0: Cybersecurity, Enterprise Risk Management, and Workforce Management Quick-Start Guide*
- Status: Final
- Published: March 2026
- Source: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/1308/final
- Relevance: Connecting cybersecurity outcomes to enterprise risk decisions.
