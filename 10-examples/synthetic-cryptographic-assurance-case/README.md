# Synthetic Cryptographic Assurance Case

> **Artifact type:** COMPLETED SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE  
> **Completion status:** Complete fictional example  
> **Operational authority:** None

This example demonstrates that the Assurance Intelligence kernel is not coupled to the AI-agent scenario.

The fictional case asks whether a service may proceed with a replacement cryptographic dependency after compatibility and rollback remediation and retest.

It demonstrates:

- one bounded human decision;
- a non-AI assurance subject;
- Observed and Tested evidence;
- a finding linked to a corrective action;
- independent retest metadata;
- verified structural closure; and
- deterministic Decision Receipt, Assurance Passport, and Executive Summary views generated from one canonical case.

All entities, evidence, findings, tests, and outcomes are synthetic and fictional. No real cryptographic implementation, customer, supplier, credential, or production system is represented.
