# Cryptographic Change Decision Pack

> **Artifact type:** TEMPLATE
> **Completion status:** Blank for reuse
> **Required for:** Completed Cryptographic Change Assurance reviews

The Cryptographic Change Decision Pack is the concise human-reviewed package for a bounded cryptographic change. It references the detailed evidence and review records rather than duplicating them.

## Recommendation

Select one package-level assurance recommendation:

- **Proceed within stated conditions**
- **Do not proceed**
- **More Evidence Required**

Record the rationale, evidence IDs, limitations, required actions, and retest conditions.

The package recommendation is not the final assurance decision. Use the repository [Review Decision](../02-evidence-manifests/review-decision-template.md) for the authorized human decision.

## Bounded Assurance Statement

State only what the reviewed evidence supports for the named scope, configuration, period, and change scenario.

Do not claim certification, formal compliance, independent operational validation, universal cryptographic safety, or applicability beyond the reviewed boundary.
