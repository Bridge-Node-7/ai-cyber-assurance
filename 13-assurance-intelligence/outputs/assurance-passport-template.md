# Assurance Passport Template

> **Artifact type:** GENERATED VIEW TEMPLATE  
> **Completion status:** Reference document  
> **Operational authority:** None

An Assurance Passport is a concise generated view of one bounded Assurance Case.

It should answer:

- what system or workflow was reviewed;
- its purpose and scope;
- accountable owner and relevant identities;
- material claims and findings;
- evidence status and important Unknowns;
- decision and conditions;
- open blocking findings;
- review expiration;
- reversal trigger.

Use bounded language such as **reviewed for stated scope** or **approved with conditions**. Do not use the passport to claim certification, universal security, compliance, or indefinite trust.
