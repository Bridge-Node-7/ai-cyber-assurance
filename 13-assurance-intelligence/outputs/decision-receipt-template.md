# Decision Receipt Template

> **Artifact type:** GENERATED VIEW TEMPLATE  
> **Completion status:** Reference document  
> **Operational authority:** None

A Decision Receipt is generated from one canonical Assurance Case.

It should show:

- decision ID and question;
- scope;
- Observed and Tested evidence;
- Reported information;
- Inferences;
- Unknowns and missing evidence;
- options considered;
- bounded decision;
- authorized human decision owner;
- conditions and corrective actions;
- confidence and basis;
- reversal trigger;
- review date and expiration;
- outcome and lessons when available.

The Decision Receipt does not replace the canonical case or grant authority by itself.
