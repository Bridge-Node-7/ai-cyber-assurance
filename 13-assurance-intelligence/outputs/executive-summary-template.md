# Executive Summary Template

> **Artifact type:** GENERATED VIEW TEMPLATE  
> **Completion status:** Reference document  
> **Operational authority:** None

The Executive Summary translates the canonical Assurance Case into decision-oriented language without changing the underlying evidence.

It should contain:

- decision question;
- why the issue matters;
- material exposure or consequence;
- strongest supporting evidence;
- important Unknowns;
- required decision;
- conditions and next actions;
- review expiration and reversal trigger.

Technical findings should be translated into bounded operational impact. The summary must not invent business impact, financial loss, probability, or confidence not supported by the case.
