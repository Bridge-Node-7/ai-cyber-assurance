## Selected wedge

**Contractor Offboarding Assurance**: identity-provider access, local applications, privileges, sessions, service identities and independent retest. Public NIST guidance explains that upstream logout may leave downstream sessions alive. This is an actionable reason to evaluate multi-system boundaries.

## Differentiation to test, not assert

The idea is not 'more cheatsheets' or unrestricted AI-generated commands. The question is whether the *shortest trustworthy path from a user's problem to a checkable, bounded, human-owned outcome* creates measurable user value. Measure task correctness, time-to-first-relevant-procedure, evidence gaps, and avoidance of unsupported PASS claims.
